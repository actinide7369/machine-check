How it works: the phone's accelerometer with gravity removed. Each dot on the top chart is the RMS (average size) of the shaking over one second. The status uses the last 3 seconds divided by the normal you recorded. WATCH starts at 1.5× normal and INSPECT at 2.5×.

Limits: the phone samples about 60 times a second, so it only sees shaking up to about half that (30 Hz, or 1,800 per minute). Faster shaking can show up at a wrong, lower speed. Amplitude isn't calibrated, so the numbers can't be compared with standards like ISO 10816 or with another phone. A loose phone or a case can change the reading, so keep it in the same spot each time.
